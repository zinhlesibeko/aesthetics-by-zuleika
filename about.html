Our Story

Aesthetics by Zuleika was founded with a simple mission: to make every client feel beautiful and confident in their own skin.

With a love for beauty and a passion for artistry, I turned my creativity into a business that celebrates individuality and self-expression.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I’m Zuleika, the face behind Aesthetics by Zuleika. I am passionate about makeup artistry and creating looks that bring out the best in every client and make special moments even more memorable.

To provide exceptional makeup services that enhance natural beauty and boost confidence for every client.

Our Vision

To become the standard makeup artist for movies, series and music videos in South Africa.
